# First Chart Story – Turning Numbers into a Narrative

**Task 7 – First Chart Story**
Veda Technology | Data Analytics Track | Level 1, Day 7

## About
I used the Sample Superstore data (2014-2017) in Excel to build 4 simple charts. Each chart answers one specific question and has a one-line takeaway. At the end, a short paragraph ties the charts together into one story.

## The 4 charts
| # | Question | Chart type | Takeaway |
|---|----------|-----------|----------|
| 1 | Which region sells the most? | Bar chart | West sells the most (725K) and South the least (392K) |
| 2 | How have sales changed over the years? | Line chart | Sales dipped slightly in 2015, then grew to 733K in 2017 |
| 3 | What share of sales does each category have? | Pie chart | Technology has the biggest share (36%), but all three categories are close |
| 4 | Which category earns the most profit? | Bar chart | Technology earns the most profit (145K) while Furniture earns only 18K |

## Why I chose these chart types
- **Bar chart** to compare categories (regions, product categories)
- **Line chart** to show a trend over time
- **Pie chart** only for a share of the whole, and only 3 slices so it stays easy to read

## The story
Sales have grown from 484K in 2014 to 733K in 2017, after a small dip in 2015. West is the biggest region and South the smallest. Sales are shared almost equally by the three categories, but profit is not: Technology earns 145K while Furniture earns only 18K. So the business is growing, but Furniture needs a closer look.
